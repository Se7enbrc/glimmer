# Choose how you play

Play from the same PC on your Mac, iPhone or iPad.
[Pair each device](GETTING_STARTED.md), then choose a game in the launcher.
iPhone and iPad are in TestFlight now, with an App Store release coming soon.

To move between devices, enable “Leave games running when you disconnect” before
streaming. Then use
[Resume or Continue here](guides/STREAMING.md#continue-a-running-game) on the
next device. Disconnecting quits the game by default.
