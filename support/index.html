Support

Connect Glimmer to Sunshine on your PC, or email for help.

Pair your PC

  1. Set up Sunshine on your PC using its official guide.
  2. Connect the PC and your iPhone or iPad to the same network.
  3. Open Glimmer and allow local network access.
  4. Select the PC, or open Settings → PCs → Add PC by address… and enter its address.
  5. Enter Glimmer's PIN in Sunshine on the PC.

Select an app in Glimmer to start streaming.

Glimmer requires iOS or iPadOS 26 or later. Games and apps run on your own PC. Glimmer does not include games, a store or a subscription catalog. To reach the same paired PC away from home, use your own VPN or tunnel address, such as Tailscale.

On Mac, follow the PC setup guide and troubleshooting guide.

Use iPhone and iPad controls

Play with a compatible controller. Touch moves the pointer; tap to click, tap with two fingers to right-click, and drag with two fingers to scroll. Tap with three fingers to show stream controls. Hold the controller's Menu button for a second and release to switch between gamepad and mouse controls.

Disconnect leaves the game running on the PC. Locking the device ends the stream on this device. Switching apps allows up to 15 seconds to return; the system may end the stream earlier.

Fix a connection or picture problem

  1. Make sure Sunshine is open on the PC and both devices are on the same network, or your own VPN is connected.
  2. If Glimmer cannot find the PC, allow local network access for Glimmer in device Settings.
  3. If the picture's shape is wrong, choose 1080p under Glimmer Settings → Quality and a matching 1920 × 1080 display mode on the PC.
  4. If sound is missing, check that the PC app is producing sound, Sunshine has audio enabled and your device's audio output and media volume are correct.

Get help or report a problem

Email glimmer-prerelease@ugfugl.io. Beta testers can also send feedback through TestFlight. Include the Glimmer version and build, device model, system version, Sunshine version, controller, audio output and steps to repeat the problem.

Do not send pairing files, private keys or PINs. Share diagnostic files only when you choose to and after reviewing them.

On iPhone and iPad, streaming uses landscape. Display and controller support depend on the PC setup.
