## 2026.10.8 (build 3, unreleased)

- Forget a PC from Settings and pair again after resetting Sunshine. Other saved
  PCs are preserved, and forgetting stays unavailable during a stream.

- Pairing no longer asks you to unlock an already available device for unrelated
  connection errors.
